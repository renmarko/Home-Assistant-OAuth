Privacy Policy

This app is used to integrate Google Nest devices with Home Assistant for private, personal home automation purposes.

  • We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.
  • All data access remains on your local network or is handled directly through Google's secure API.
  • This app is not intended for public or commercial use.
  • OAuth credentials are used solely to authorize your Home Assistant instance to access your own Google Nest devices.
